About

I'm a mobile developer based in İzmir, Türkiye, focused on building consumer apps that people open every single day. My main craft is Flutter, taken well beyond the basics — native iOS widgets and Live Activities in SwiftUI, Android home-screen widgets, and full companion apps for Apple Watch and Wear OS.

Day to day I care about the things users feel but never see: cold-start time, smooth scrolling on low-end devices, offline reliability and crash-free sessions. I also work close to the product side — analytics, A/B-driven onboarding, ads and subscription monetization.
